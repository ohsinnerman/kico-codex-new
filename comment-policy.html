Comment policy

KiCo welcomes useful criticism and respectful disagreement in spaces where comments are enabled.

Last updated: 3 November 2026.

Keep discussion respectful

Talk about ideas without attacking other commenters. Disagreement is welcome when it stays respectful. Please do not post abusive, threatening, harassing or personally identifying information about someone else.

Moderation

Comments are moderated openly. KiCo may remove content that breaks this policy and may block a user who repeatedly posts abuse or otherwise disrupts discussion.

KiCo may remove comments containing defamation, bigotry, hate, sexism, profanity, affiliate links, keyword-stuffed or link-bait names, or other material that is irrelevant or harmful to the discussion.

Contact

Questions about moderation can be sent to admin@kicoedtech.xyz.
