The full syllabus

This proposed order moves from foundations to live campaign work. Choose a start date to see the stage dates and the Friday finish date for week 12.

Choose a start date to see your proposed course dates.

  1. Core marketing concepts

    Learn customer needs and behaviour, the 4Ps, segmentation, targeting, positioning, value, satisfaction, brand identity and loyalty.

  2. Digital marketing fundamentals

    Explore digital marketing principles, brand audits and personas, career and marketing plans, objectives, key elements and a case study.

  3. Google Workspace

    Use Docs, Sheets and Slides together, organise Drive, schedule with Calendar, communicate in Gmail and Meet, and explore integrations and automation.

  4. Website planning and creation

    Set website goals, choose a platform, plan its structure, design for usable mobile experiences and cover domain, hosting and security basics.

  5. WordPress

    Set up a WordPress site, shape its theme and branding, manage pages and media, choose useful plugins, and keep the site backed up, updated and fast.

  6. Domain, hosting and setup

    Compare domains, hosting and builders, register a suitable name, connect hosting to a CMS and configure SSL and basic security.

  1. Landing page optimisation

    Understand conversion goals, shape a clear mobile-friendly page, write useful copy and avoid common landing-page mistakes.

  2. Search engine optimisation

    Learn how search engines work, research keywords, improve on-page content and build off-page authority through links and brand mentions.

  3. Content marketing

    Plan blogs, video, infographics, case studies and social posts around audience needs, then distribute, measure and repurpose the work.

  4. Inbound marketing

    Compare inbound and outbound, attract visitors with content and SEO, turn visits into leads, nurture them by email and automation, and track results.

  5. Graphic designing

    Apply design principles and tools to posts, ads, banners, thumbnails, logos and brand kits, then prepare visuals for web and print.

  1. Social media marketing

    Choose platforms for a goal, create engaging content, build a community and plan paid social campaigns.

  2. Facebook marketing

    Set up a Business Page, publish posts, stories, reels and lives, run targeted ads, and use Pixel, Insights and Ads Manager.

  3. Instagram marketing

    Improve a profile, plan content around trends and hashtags, work with creators and user-generated content, run Meta ads and read Insights.

  4. LinkedIn marketing

    Improve personal and company pages, publish thought leadership, grow a network, use LinkedIn Ads and measure B2B lead activity.

  5. Quora marketing

    Build a credible profile, answer high-traffic questions with useful stories, explore Quora Ads and use analytics to refine activity.

  6. Local promotion

    Improve a Google Business Profile and map listing, use local SEO, social, WhatsApp and ads, and explore partnerships, creators and events.

  7. Video marketing

    Choose video formats, use storytelling, distribute across platforms, plan paid video ads and measure performance.

  1. E-commerce marketing

    Set up a store and product pages, attract visitors through search, ads and social, recover abandoned carts, compare marketplaces and track sales and ROI.

  2. App store optimisation and mobile

    Improve app titles, descriptions, keywords and visuals, understand ratings and reviews, plan mobile campaigns and promote installs.

  3. Email marketing

    Build and segment a list, write subject lines and email copy, design mobile templates, automate campaigns and track opens, clicks, conversions and unsubscribes.

  4. Online lead generation

    Use lead magnets, focused landing pages and forms, paid and organic acquisition, then manage and nurture leads in a CRM.

  5. Online reputation management

    Monitor mentions and reviews, respond to criticism, invite honest positive reviews and build credibility through PR, blogs and social content.

  6. Google AdSense

    Review eligibility and setup, place ads with care, understand CPC, CPM and CTR, and read AdSense and Analytics reports without losing sight of user experience.

  7. Digital payment systems

    Compare payment methods and gateways, learn about encryption, OTPs and fraud protection, and review contactless, buy-now-pay-later and crypto trends.

  1. Web analytics

    Set up and use GA4, understand key metrics and tracked behaviour, and turn activity into clear dashboards and reports.

  2. Digital marketing tools

    Explore tools for SEO, social publishing, email and automation, analytics and tracking, and advertising.

  3. Reports and proposals

    Learn report types, proposal essentials, useful data visualisation and tools for clear client communication.

  4. Internship and live projects

    Apply the work to client projects and campaigns, practise teamwork and problem-solving, and prepare case studies and reports for a portfolio.

This five-stage, 12-week order is a proposal for KiCo and the client to confirm.
