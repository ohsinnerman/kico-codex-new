Privacy policy

This policy explains what information KiCo may receive when you visit kicoedtech.xyz or contact us about a course.

Last updated: 3 November 2026.

Information you provide

KiCo receives personal information only when you choose to provide it, for example when you ask about a course. This may include your name, email address, phone number, city, state and preferred start date. You may browse the site without submitting these details.

The new enquiry form does not store information on this website and there is no enquiry database behind it. When you submit the form, your browser opens WhatsApp with a message for you to review and send. If you choose the email fallback, your email app opens a draft. WhatsApp, your email provider and your device handle those messages under their own terms and privacy practices.

Technical information and cookies

Like many websites, the site may receive non-personal technical information such as browser type, device and operating system details, and information about how a visit reached the site.

Cookies may be used to remember preferences, understand aggregate site use or support advertising. You can set your browser to refuse cookies or to alert you when one is set. Some site or advertising features may then work differently.

How information is used

  • To personalise the website experience.
  • To understand site use in aggregate and improve the website and services.
  • To respond to questions and provide customer support.
  • To reply to an enquiry at the email address or phone number you provide.

Security and sharing

KiCo uses reasonable safeguards intended to protect information it handles against unauthorised access, change, disclosure or loss. No website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

KiCo does not sell, trade or rent personal information. Non-identifying aggregate information may be shared with business partners, trusted affiliates or advertising partners to understand general site use or support services.

Advertising partners

Advertising partners, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to show and measure advertising. Their use is governed by their own privacy terms. You can review or change Google ad personalisation choices in Google Ads Settings.

Policy changes

KiCo may update this policy as the site or services change. The revised policy will appear on this page with an updated date. Please check it periodically.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to admin@kicoedtech.xyz.
